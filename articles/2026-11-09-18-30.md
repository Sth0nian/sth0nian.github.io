# Day 1 of 21 - The hardest 5km I've run in a while
running, habits

So much for "promise today, then try that again tomorrow".

It turns out "tomorrow" is a very flexible word when nobody is keeping score. So I am keeping score again. Back in June last year I wrote about a wise nugget of wisdom from the Data Architect on my team: it takes 21 days to form a habit, 4 days to break it. Last year I used it as the minimum to get through January. I proved the second half of it all on my own in 2026. This time it is the whole plan. 21 runs, 21 days in a row, and see what is left standing at the end.

Today was day 1. Here is what Strava has to say about it:

**5.02km** in **24:51**, which works out at **4:57 per km**.
**29m** of climbing, which in the Netherlands counts as hill training.

On paper that is a perfectly respectable run. It is faster than my average for the whole of 2025 (5:18 per km), which is probably the problem. Nobody told my legs that we were starting again from scratch. Somewhere in my head I am still the person who ran 14km a day last December, and that person apparently thought 5km was a warm-up. My lungs disagreed from about the second kilometre onwards, and by the fourth they had taken the matter up with management.

It was hard. Not "last January" hard, I don't think anything will ever be that hard again, but hard enough to remind me that fitness is a subscription, not a one-off purchase. And I let mine lapse.

The conversation before the run was back as well. I had sort of missed it, in the way you miss an annoying colleague who moved to another team.

King : Right. Day 1 of 21.

Inner-voice King : 21? Last time you only promised one day at a time.

King : And "one day" kept moving to tomorrow. So now it has a number. Numbers I can do.

Inner-voice King : It's November. It's dark. It's cold.

King : It was dark and cold last November too, and I ran 13km a day.

Inner-voice King : And look where that got you. :devil:

King : ...here, apparently. Shoes are on. Bye.

So that is the deal. One run a day for 21 days. I am not chasing distance or pace, and I am definitely going to try to run slower than 4:57 tomorrow on purpose. The only thing that matters is that there is a run on the board every day until the 29th of November. If the nugget of wisdom holds, by then I will stop having that conversation again. If it doesn't, at least I will have 21 data points to make a graph out of, and you know how I feel about graphs.

Day 2 is tomorrow. Probably slower. Hopefully with fewer complaints from management.

See you on day 21.
