Lab 03

Notifications & badge

Local notifications shown by the service worker, and the number on the app icon.

Install to test. Notifications and the app badge only exist in the installed app on iOS. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open PWA Lab from your home screen.

🔔 Permission

iOS only shows the prompt in response to a tap, and only asks once. If you deny it, re-enable it in Settings → Notifications → PWA Lab.

✉️ Send a local notification

Delays use a timer inside the page. iOS pauses the app soon after you leave it, so a delayed notification may only arrive when you come back. Reliable scheduled notifications need Web Push.

🔴 App icon badge

Sets the red number on the home screen icon. Needs notification permission.
